HAUNTED FOREST — how to start in ROBLOX
========================================

1) Open Roblox Studio
   - Go to https://create.roblox.com
   - Click Create / open Studio
   - New experience → Baseplate (or your forest map)

2) Build a quick forest (or use your map)
   - Add trees / parts in Workspace
   - Optional: rename the place “Haunted Forest”

3) Put your animatronic models in Workspace
   Make 3 Models named EXACTLY:
     PhantomPuppet
     Ennard
     WitheredBonnie
   Each model needs a Humanoid + HumanoidRootPart (Rig / NPC).
   Put them where you want them to spawn (or near SpawnLocation).

4) Add beacons (survivor objectives)
   - Make 4 Parts named: Beacon1, Beacon2, Beacon3, Beacon4
   - Put them around the map
   - Color them dark green for now

5) Paste the scripts (from this roblox folder)
   ServerScriptService:
     - ChoosePhantomPuppet.lua (Script)  ← random player becomes Phantom Puppet
     - RoleSetup.lua           (Script)
     - RoundTimer.lua          (Script)
     - KillerAI.lua            (Script)  ← leave OFF if you want player killer only
     - BeaconServer.lua        (Script)
     - PhantomPuppetM1_Server.lua (Script)

   StarterPlayer → StarterPlayerScripts:
     - RoleReveal.lua          (LocalScript)  ← "YOU ARE PHANTOM PUPPET" splash
     - PhantomPuppetM1_Client.lua (LocalScript)
     - HudClient.lua           (LocalScript)
     - BeaconClient.lua        (LocalScript)

   Put your PhantomPuppet model in ServerStorage (or Workspace).
   Optional: Workspace part named KillerSpawn (where the killer appears).

6) Press PLAY (blue Play button at the top of Studio)
   - After a few seconds: "CHOOSING KILLER"
   - One random player becomes Phantom Puppet (the rest are Survivors)
   - Killer can click to M1 swing
   - Clock goes 12:00 AM → 6:00 AM
   - Hold E near a Beacon to light it

7) Publish to Roblox (so friends can join)
   - File → Publish to Roblox
   - Set name / description
   - Home → your experience → Play

TIPS
- If killers don’t move: check model names and that they have HumanoidRootPart.
- If HUD doesn’t show: HudClient must be a LocalScript under StarterPlayerScripts.
- Timer style is Bite by Night–like (12 AM → 6 AM).
