ROB 67s FROM CAYLUS — how to start in ROBLOX
=============================================

Like Steal a Brainrot + Caylus's game, but the collectibles are 67s.
No horror. No fighting. If Caylus tags you, you just drop the 67.

1) Open Roblox Studio
   - New experience → Baseplate
   - Save as: Rob 67s From Caylus  (or Steal a 67)

2) Paste the 2 scripts

   ServerScriptService:
     - Server.lua          (Script)

   StarterPlayer → StarterPlayerScripts:
     - Client.lua          (LocalScript)

3) Press PLAY
   The map builds itself:
     - red conveyor in the middle (67s slide by — buy them)
     - 4 player bases
     - CAYLUS VAULT (north) with free steal 67s
     - Caylus NPC who chases thieves

4) How to play (Caylus / Steal a Brainrot loop)
   - You get a base + $100
   - Stand by the CONVEYOR and hold E on a 67 to BUY it
   - 67s on your pads print cash every second
   - Hold E on someone else's 67 to STEAL it (if their base is unlocked)
   - Or sneak into CAYLUS VAULT and steal his 67s for free
   - RUN HOME — you are slower while carrying
   - Caylus chases you. If he touches you, the 67 goes back
   - Reach your empty pad and it places
   - Hold E on LOCK so other players cannot steal from you
   - Hold E on the gold REBIRTH button on your base
   - Rebirth wipes your 67s + cash, gives +1x money forever
   - Rebirth 7 = 7-JUMP (jump 7 times in the air)

5) Rebirth costs
   Rebirth 1 = $200
   Rebirth 2 = $400
   ...
   Rebirth 7 = $1400  ← unlocks 7-JUMP
   After that you can keep rebirthing for more cash multiplier

6) 67 rarities on the conveyor
   Common 67     cheap, common
   Rare 67       blue
   Epic 67       purple
   Secret 67     gold, rare
   Vault 67      only in Caylus's base (steal only)
   Caylus 67     best one, only in his vault

7) Decorate later (the building / obby part)
   Recolor floors, add walls, neon, signs.
   Do NOT delete: Plot1-4, Pad1-4, LockButton, RebirthButton, Conveyor, CaylusBase, Caylus

TIPS
- Test chase: steal from the vault, then run to your base
- Test 2 players: Home → Test → 2 Players
- If HUD missing: Client.lua must be a LocalScript under StarterPlayerScripts
- If map missing: Server.lua must be a Script under ServerScriptService
